Thermal Comfort Shelter Design

AI-powered software model for area-specific shelter optimization with 3D visualization

Problem Statement

Traditional shelter designs often fail to account for regional climate variations, resulting in inadequate thermal comfort for residents. Different geographical areas experience diverse weather patterns, temperature fluctuations, and environmental conditions that demand tailored shelter solutions.

❌ Issue 1

One-size-fits-all shelter designs don't adapt to local climate conditions

❌ Issue 2

Lack of data-driven approach to thermal comfort optimization

❌ Issue 3

Difficulty in predicting thermal performance across different regions

Our Solution

We've developed an intelligent, AI-powered software model that analyzes area-specific climate data and designs shelters optimized for thermal comfort maintenance. Our system takes into account local temperature patterns, humidity, wind conditions, and solar radiation to create custom shelter designs with 3D visualization.
